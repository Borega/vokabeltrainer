## Passiv
Merksatz: Form von be + Past Participle: Das Objekt wird zum Subjekt.
Erklärung:
Bildung: be (in der passenden Zeit) + Past Participle.
Present: is/are made. Past: was/were made. Future: will be made. Present Perfect: has/have been made.
Der Handelnde steht mit by (by a famous author).
Aufgaben:
English *is spoken* (speak) all over the world.
! Passiv Present: am/is/are + Past Participle.
The window *was broken* (break) by the boy.
! Passiv Past: was/were + Past Participle.
These cars *are made* (make) in Germany.
! Passiv Present, Plural: are + Past Participle.
The letter *was written* (write) yesterday.
! Passiv Past: was + Past Participle.
The houses *were built* (build) in 1990.
! Passiv Past, Plural: were + Past Participle.
The cake *was eaten* (eat) by the children.
! Past Participle von eat ist eaten.
The homework *is done* (do) every day.
! Past Participle von do ist done.
The room *is being cleaned* (clean) at the moment.
! Passiv Present Continuous: am/is/are being + Past Participle.
The match *was cancelled* (cancel) because of the rain.
! Passiv Past: was + Past Participle.
The bridge *will be built* (build) next year.
! Passiv Future: will be + Past Participle.
The problem has *been solved* (solve) already.
! Passiv Present Perfect: has/have been + Past Participle.
The book was written {by|from|of} a famous author.
! Handelnde Person im Passiv mit by.
Active: They clean the room. Passive: The room *is* cleaned.
! Aktiv „They clean the room“ wird zu „The room is cleaned“.
Active: She wrote a poem. Passive: A poem *was* written by her.
! Past bleibt Past: wrote wird zu was written.
Active: Someone stole my bike. Passive: My bike *was* stolen.
! Passiv: Objekt wird zum Subjekt, Verb wird zu was + Past Participle.
Active: People speak Spanish in Mexico. Passive: Spanish *is spoken* in Mexico.
! Aktiv Present wird zu is/are + Past Participle.
*Was* the window opened by Tom?
! Frage im Passiv: was/is/are vor das Subjekt.
The documents *were not found|weren’t found* (find).
! Verneinung im Passiv Past: was/were + not + Past Participle.
