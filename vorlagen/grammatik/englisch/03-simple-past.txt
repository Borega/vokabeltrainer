## Simple Past
Merksatz: Regelmäßige Verben: -ed. Unregelmäßige Verben haben eigene Formen.
Erklärung:
Das Simple Past benutzt man für abgeschlossene Handlungen in der Vergangenheit.
Regelmäßig: Grundform + -ed (study → studied, stop → stopped).
Unregelmäßig: eigene Form (go → went, see → saw).
Fragen und Verneinung: did (not) + Grundform.
Signalwörter: yesterday, last week, ago.
Aufgaben:
Yesterday I *walked* (walk) to school.
! Regelmäßige Verben bilden das Simple Past mit -ed.
She *studied* (study) hard last week.
! Konsonant + y wird zu -ied.
We *stopped* (stop) at the red light.
! Kurzer Vokal + Konsonant: Konsonant wird verdoppelt.
He *went* (go) to London last year.
! Unregelmäßig: go – went – gone.
I *saw* (see) a great film yesterday.
! Unregelmäßig: see – saw – seen.
They *bought* (buy) a new car in May.
! Unregelmäßig: buy – bought – bought.
She *ate* (eat) pizza for lunch.
! Unregelmäßig: eat – ate – eaten.
*Did* you watch TV last night?
! Fragen im Simple Past mit did + Grundform.
I *did not|didn’t* (know) the answer.
! Nach did not / didn't steht die Grundform.
We *had* (have) a lot of fun at the party.
! Unregelmäßig: have – had – had.
My mother *gave* (give) me a present.
! Unregelmäßig: give – gave – given.
He *wrote* (write) a letter to his friend.
! Unregelmäßig: write – wrote – written.
When *did* you arrive?
! Fragewort + did + Subjekt + Grundform.
It *was* (be) very cold yesterday.
! be im Simple Past: I/he/she/it was.
We *were* (be) at the cinema last night.
! be im Simple Past: we/you/they were.
She *took* (take) the bus to work.
! Unregelmäßig: take – took – taken.
