## Steigerung der Adjektive
Merksatz: Kurz: -er/-est. Lang: more/most. Unregelmäßig: good – better – best.
Erklärung:
Einsilbige Adjektive: -er / the -est (Verdopplung bei kurzem Vokal: big → bigger).
Konsonant + y: -ier / -iest.
Lange Adjektive: more / the most.
Unregelmäßig: good – better – best, bad – worse – worst.
Vergleich: than; Gleichheit: as … as.
Aufgaben:
My brother is *taller* (tall) than me.
! Einsilbig: -er.
He is *the tallest* (tall) boy in our class.
! Einsilbig im Superlativ: the + -est.
This bag is *bigger* (big) than that one.
! Kurzer Vokal + Konsonant: Konsonant wird verdoppelt.
Russia is *the biggest* (big) country in the world.
! Kurzer Vokal + Konsonant: Konsonant wird verdoppelt.
She is *happier* (happy) today than yesterday.
! Konsonant + y wird zu -ier.
It was *the happiest* (happy) day of my life.
! Konsonant + y wird zu -iest.
This street is *nicer* (nice) than the other one.
! Adjektive auf -e bekommen nur -r.
That is *the nicest* (nice) café in town.
! Adjektive auf -e bekommen nur -st.
This flower is *more beautiful* (beautiful) than that one.
! Lange Adjektive: more + Adjektiv.
This is *the most beautiful* (beautiful) picture in the museum.
! Lange Adjektive: the most + Adjektiv.
This book is *more interesting* (interesting) than the film.
! Lange Adjektive: more + Adjektiv.
Your idea is *better* (good) than mine.
! Unregelmäßig: good – better – best.
She is *the best* (good) player in the team.
! Unregelmäßig: good – better – best.
The weather today is *worse* (bad) than yesterday.
! Unregelmäßig: bad – worse – worst.
This was *the worst* (bad) film I have ever seen.
! Unregelmäßig: bad – worse – worst.
My bike is faster {than|as|then} yours.
! Vergleich mit Komparativ: than.
She is as tall *as* her brother.
! Gleichheit: as + Adjektiv + as.
Mount Everest is *the* highest mountain in the world.
! Der Superlativ steht immer mit the.
He is not *as* old *as* my father.
! Verneinte Gleichheit: not as … as.
