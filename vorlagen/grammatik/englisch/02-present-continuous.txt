## Present Continuous
Merksatz: am/is/are + Verb-ing: Handlung, die gerade passiert.
Erklärung:
Das Present Continuous benutzt man für Handlungen im Moment des Sprechens, vorübergehende Situationen und feste Pläne.
Bildung: am/is/are + Verb mit -ing (write → writing, run → running, lie → lying).
Signalwörter: now, at the moment, look, listen.
Aufgaben:
I *am reading* (read) a book right now.
! Present Continuous: am/is/are + Verb-ing für Handlungen, die gerade passieren.
She *is cooking* (cook) dinner at the moment.
! she/he/it + is + Verb-ing.
They *are playing* (play) football now.
! we/you/they + are + Verb-ing.
*Are* you listening to me?
! Frage: are/is/am vor das Subjekt stellen.
He *is running* (run) in the park.
! Kurzer Vokal + Konsonant: Konsonant wird verdoppelt (run – running).
We *are writing* (write) a test today.
! Stummes -e fällt weg (write – writing).
The children *are not sleeping|aren’t sleeping* (sleep, verneint).
! Verneinung: is/are/am + not + Verb-ing.
Look! It *is raining* (rain).
! Signalwörter: look, listen, now, at the moment.
What *are* you doing?
! Fragewort + are/is/am + Subjekt + Verb-ing.
I *am meeting* (meet) my friends tomorrow.
! Present Continuous auch für feste Pläne in der Zukunft.
My brother *is swimming* (swim) in the lake.
! Kurzer Vokal + m: Verdopplung (swim – swimming).
Be quiet! The baby *is sleeping* (sleep).
! Present Continuous bei Handlungen im Moment des Sprechens.
She *is lying* (lie) on the sofa.
! -ie wird zu -y vor -ing (lie – lying).
Listen! Someone *is singing* (sing).
! Signalwort listen: Handlung läuft gerade.
We *are learning* (learn) English this year.
! Auch für vorübergehende Situationen (this week, this year).
