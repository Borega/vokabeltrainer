## To be (sein)
Merksatz: I am, you are, he/she/it is, we/you/they are – Vergangenheit: was/were.
Erklärung:
Present: I am, you are, he/she/it is, we are, you are, they are.
Past: I/he/she/it was, you/we/they were.
Verneinung: be + not (isn’t, aren’t, wasn’t, weren’t). Frage: be vor das Subjekt (Are you ready? Was she late?).
Kurzformen: I’m, you’re, he’s, she’s, it’s, we’re, they’re.
Aufgaben:
I *am* (be) twelve years old.
! I → am.
She *is* (be) my best friend.
! he/she/it → is.
We *are* (be) in the same class.
! we/you/they → are.
My parents *are* (be) at work.
! Mehrzahl (they) → are.
The dog *is* (be) in the garden.
! it → is.
You *are* (be) very kind.
! you → are.
*Are* you hungry?
! Frage: be steht vor dem Subjekt.
*Is* he your brother?
! Frage mit he/she/it: Is.
*Am* I late?
! Frage mit I: Am.
He *is not|isn’t* (be) at home today.
! Verneinung: be + not.
We *are not|aren’t* (be) tired.
! Verneinung: are + not.
I *am not|’m not* (be) from Spain.
! Bei I gibt es nur: I am not / I’m not (nicht: I amn’t).
Where *is* (be) my bag?
! Singular (bag) → is.
Where *are* (be) my keys?
! Mehrzahl (keys) → are.
Yesterday I *was* (be) ill.
! Past: I/he/she/it → was.
We *were* (be) at the cinema last night.
! Past: we/you/they → were.
They *were not|weren’t* (be) at school yesterday.
! Past, Verneinung: were + not.
*Was* it cold last week?
! Frage im Past mit it: Was.
*Were* you at the party?
! Frage im Past mit you: Were.
It *was* (be) a great day.
! Past mit it: was.
How old *are* (be) you?
! how old + are + you.
There *are* (be) three books on the table.
! there + are bei Mehrzahl.
There *is* (be) a cat under the bed.
! there + is bei Einzahl.
