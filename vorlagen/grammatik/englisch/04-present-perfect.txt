## Present Perfect
Merksatz: have/has + Past Participle: Ergebnis zählt jetzt noch.
Erklärung:
Das Present Perfect verbindet Vergangenheit und Gegenwart.
Bildung: have/has + Past Participle.
Signalwörter: already, just, yet, ever, never, since (Zeitpunkt), for (Zeitspanne).
Aufgaben:
I *have finished* (finish) my homework.
! Present Perfect: have/has + Past Participle.
She *has visited* (visit) Paris twice.
! she/he/it + has + Past Participle.
We *have seen* (see) that film before.
! Unregelmäßig: see – saw – seen.
He *has eaten* (eat) breakfast already.
! Signalwort already: Present Perfect.
*Have* you ever been to Spain?
! Frage: Have/Has + Subjekt + Past Participle.
I have *lost* (lose) my keys.
! Past Participle von lose ist lost.
They have never *been* (be) to Canada.
! Past Participle von be ist been, never steht zwischen have und Participle.
She has just *gone* (go) out.
! Past Participle von go ist gone.
We haven't *met* (meet) him yet.
! Signalwort yet in Verneinungen und Fragen.
I have lived here *since* 2020.
! since + Zeitpunkt (since 2020, since Monday).
He has worked here *for* five years.
! for + Zeitspanne (for five years, for a week).
How long *have* you known her?
! How long + have/has + Subjekt + Past Participle.
My dad has *written* (write) three books.
! Past Participle von write ist written.
She has *done* (do) her homework.
! Past Participle von do ist done.
I *have broken* (break) my arm. It still hurts.
! Present Perfect, wenn das Ergebnis jetzt noch relevant ist.
It has *been* (be) a long day.
! Past Participle von be ist been.
