## Have got / has got (haben)
Merksatz: I/you/we/they have got, he/she/it has got.
Erklärung:
have got bedeutet „haben/besitzen“ und wird wie have verwendet, vor allem im britischen Englisch.
Bejahte Sätze: I/you/we/they have got, he/she/it has got (Kurzform: I’ve got, he’s got).
Verneinung: haven’t got / hasn’t got. Frage: Have you got …? Has she got …?
Kurzantwort: Yes, I have. / No, he hasn’t.
Aufgaben:
I *have* got a new bike.
! I → have got.
She *has* got long hair.
! he/she/it → has got.
We *have* got a big garden.
! we → have got.
My brother *has* got a dog.
! Er (brother) → has got.
They *have* got two cars.
! they → have got.
You *have* got a nice room.
! you → have got.
The house *has* got four bedrooms.
! it → has got.
*Have* you got a pen?
! Frage: have/has vor dem Subjekt.
*Has* she got a sister?
! Frage mit he/she/it: Has.
*Has* the school got a library?
! Frage mit it: Has.
I *have not|haven’t* got any money.
! Verneinung: have not got.
He *has not|hasn’t* got a phone.
! Verneinung mit he/she/it: has not got.
We *have not|haven’t* got any homework today.
! Verneinung: have not got.
Yes, I *have*. (Have you got a ruler?)
! Kurzantwort: Yes, I have.
No, she *has not|hasn’t*. (Has she got a brother?)
! Kurzantwort: No, she hasn’t.
My sister *has* got blue eyes.
! Er/sie/es: has got.
How many brothers *have* you got?
! how many + have + Subjekt + got.
How many pets *has* he got?
! how many + has + he/she/it + got.
They *have not|haven’t* got a car.
! Verneinung: have not got.
*Have* we got any milk?
! Frage mit we: Have.
My parents *have* got a holiday house.
! Mehrzahl (parents) → have got.
